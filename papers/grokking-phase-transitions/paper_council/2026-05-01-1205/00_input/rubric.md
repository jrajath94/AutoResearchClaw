# Rubric — NeurIPS 2026

Use this rubric for papers targeting NeurIPS. Each dimension is 1-10 with the
anchors given. Strong Accept threshold: ≥7 across most dimensions. Reject
threshold: ≤4 on any single dimension is a rejection trigger.

## Dimensions

### 1. Originality / Novelty (weight: 1.0)
- 10: A new paradigm; the field will reorganize around this
- 8: Substantial conceptual advance over the closest prior work
- 6: Clear improvement / extension; combines existing ideas in non-obvious ways
- 4: Incremental; the contribution is engineering, not science
- 2: The contribution exists in prior work the authors didn't cite

### 2. Soundness (weight: 1.5 — heaviest)
- 10: Methodology is the field standard or sets a new bar; no concerns
- 8: Strong methodology; minor improvements possible
- 6: Adequate methodology; some concerns flagged but not fatal
- 4: Methodology has serious gaps that compromise main claims
- 2: Methodology is fundamentally flawed; results untrustworthy

### 3. Significance (weight: 1.0)
- 10: Will change practice / theory across multiple subfields
- 8: Important within the subfield; will be cited heavily
- 6: Useful contribution within a niche
- 4: Limited utility beyond the specific demo
- 2: Solves a problem nobody is working on

### 4. Clarity (weight: 0.7)
- 10: Could be a textbook chapter
- 8: Clear, well-organized, easy to follow
- 6: Mostly clear; some sections require re-reading
- 4: Significant clarity issues; key methods unclear
- 2: I couldn't follow the paper

### 5. Reproducibility (weight: 1.0)
- 10: Code, data, configs, seeds all released; verified working
- 8: Code+configs released; data accessible
- 6: Sufficient detail in paper to reproduce
- 4: Critical hyperparameters or code missing
- 2: Cannot be reproduced from what's provided

### 6. Contextualization vs prior work (weight: 0.8)
- 10: Comprehensive related work; novel framing of the gap
- 8: Strong coverage; correctly positioned vs SOTA
- 6: Adequate coverage; some misses
- 4: Significant prior work missing
- 2: Reinvents prior work without acknowledgment

### 7. Ethical / Broader Impact (weight: 0.5)
- 10: Thoughtful, specific, balanced impact statement
- 8: Adequate coverage
- 6: Boilerplate
- 4: Inadequate
- 2: Concerns not addressed

## Decision boundaries
- Strong Accept: weighted avg ≥ 8.0
- Accept: 6.5-8.0
- Borderline: 5.5-6.5
- Reject: 4.0-5.5
- Strong Reject: < 4.0

## NeurIPS 2026 reviewer guidelines
"Reviewers should be technically rigorous, intellectually generous, and write
reviews that help authors improve their work whether the paper is accepted or
not. A great review identifies the strongest version of the paper's
contribution and the most fundamental concern with it."
